Overview

This tool drafts the CNX Curative Action Report (CAR) in the official Word form and the matching Pad Summary rows (Title-Curative and Ownership tabs) from the title opinion, Title Mapping Curative, internal bringdown, abstract and long-form leases.

It is a first draft, not a finished CAR. Every recommendation the AI writes appears in red in the Word file — the same way analyst entries are marked — so the reviewer can see exactly what to confirm.
Your documents stay in your browser. Text is read from text-based PDFs on your own computer; only the extracted text is sent for analysis. Only scanned (image-only) PDFs are uploaded, temporarily, for OCR.

Step 2 — Upload Documents

DocumentWhat it drives
Title Opinion(s) (PDF — select several if there is more than one)RequiredHeader, leasehold, encumbrances, taxes, Miscellaneous section and every curative item. All opinions are listed oldest → newest in the header and each gets its own curative table; ownership comes from the newest.
Title Mapping Curative (PDF)FINAL Resolved Acreage (read straight from the "Resolved Mapping Acreage" line on page 1), resolved acres per parcel, outsales, and survey/acreage clouds. If the file contains an original and a revised TMC, page 1 (the revised one) is used.
Internal Bringdown(s) (.doc, .docx or PDF — select several if there is more than one)One "Internal Bringdown" row per bringdown in the header (oldest → newest, ordered by the date in the file name), mortgage satisfactions, and any new conveyances, leases or ownership changes after the opinion.
Abstract of Title (PDF)Wells — every well on the abstract's well sheet (including nearby, plugged and expired/not-drilled wells), combined with the wells in the title opinion(s), duplicates (same API #) merged. Only the cover pages and well pages are used. If the well sheet is a scanned image (e.g. some Daxton Irving abstracts), it is read from the page image.
Long Form Lease(s) (PDF)Agreement terms in the CAR ownership table and the Pad Summary Ownership tab — dates, extensions, royalty, deduct language, pooling, Pugh, etc. Upload one file per lease.
Big files are fine. A 140-page opinion or 230-page abstract is read in a few seconds in your browser.
Scanned or handwritten leases (no usable text layer, e.g. old cursive leases) are read page-by-page from the image by the AI. Expect roughly 20–40 seconds per 4 pages; the checklist shows the page count and a timer. Check names, dates and acreage on these — handwriting can be misread, and unclear words are marked [?].

Step 3 — Generate

1
Read the documents

Text is pulled from each PDF and Word file; scanned leases are OCR'd.

2
Read each lease

Each lease is analyzed on its own for the Pad Summary lease columns.

3
Build the CAR and Pad Summary

The analyses run at once: header/encumbrances, the leasehold chain (amendments, every assignment, ORRI), wells from the opinion, wells from the abstract, specific curative items, general items & limitations, and owners by parcel. Expect 2–4 minutes.

The checklist under the button shows each step. If one step fails, the rest still finish — fill that section by hand or run it again.

Step 4 — Review the CAR

Every section of the CAR form appears as an editable panel, in the same order as the Word document. Tables have + Add row and Delete; empty tables print "N/A".

Page-1 clouds: each numbered line in a Curative Summary box prints as its own row in the CAR.
Outsales and wells list everything from the title opinion(s) plus the TMC (outsales) or abstract (wells), with duplicates removed — outsales are listed whether they are before or after the lease.
Ownership changes after the opinion: if the bringdown shows a deed that changes vesting, the owners are updated and a note appears under "Notes for reviewer" — confirm it.
Ownership tables show Exec and Royalty interests as the fraction with the decimal right below it in the same box. WI tables stay in decimals, with one row per WI owner (several leases to the same owner are added together).
Assignment chain lists every instrument in the opinion's lease chain, including assignments the opinion gave no effect (noted in Other Restrictions) — delete any you don't want on the report.
Check the Curative Summary last. It is built from the items marked as open, but once you edit recommendations below it does not rebuild — update it to match.
Curative items: left box is the title defect copied from the opinion, right box is the CNX Recommendation/Status. Compare the defect text against the opinion for anything cut off at page breaks.

Step 5 — Review the Pad Summary

Title-Curative tab: one row with the opinion date, title search certified date ("(BD)" when a bringdown was done), heirship, and the open Land / Third Party / Mapping / Title action items from page 1 of the CAR.

Ownership tab: one row per owner per tract, columns A through BR exactly as in the unit Pad Summary. Unleased owners get "OPEN" in the lease columns. Executive and royalty interests are entered as fractions (=1/2) and the acreage / NRI columns are Excel formulas.

Fill column AH (Resolved Gross Tract Acres in Unit) from the unit plat after pasting — the tool can't know how much of the tract is inside the unit.

Recommendation Wording

The AI follows the wording used on prior CARs:

Opinion itemTypical recommendation
Survey / plats don't close (TMC provided)Satisfied by Title Mapping Curative
Roads / centerlineAdvisory. Typically, the oil and gas runs to the centerline of an adjacent roadway unless otherwise specifically stated.
No current CNX leaseLeasehold is open. Land to execute a new lease with current oil and gas owners… and place of record in ___ County.
Old unreleased leases, no wells foundSatisfied. A review of PADEP and ___ Recorder indicates that there are no old wells or leases holding the subject parcel…
Mortgage released in bringdownSatisfied. Mtg was satisfied and released in ___
Old gaps / tax sale recordsWaived based on passage of time and lack of adverse claims against the subject parcels.
Aliases, general items, non-action itemsAdvisory

Known Limitations

Instrument numbers for releases are often not in the bringdown text — look them up and add them to the Liens table.
"Lease in Other CNX Units?", BA #, QDO codes and Land Ops / D.O. approval columns are internal CNX data and are left blank.
Scanned leases are read by the AI from the page images — no extra setup needed.
Unusual opinion layouts (e.g. older Steptoe & Johnson opinions with requirements at the front and advisory items at the back, or Kostrub's Roman-numeral items) may need curative items finished by hand — compare them against the opinion. If an opinion has no general/non-action section, delete anything that appears there.

FAQ

Can I change the CAR template?
Yes — replace templates/car-template.docx in the GitHub repo with the new form. Keep the table headings the same so the tool can find each table.
There are hundreds of owners — will it work?
Yes. Owners are extracted in one compact pass and each owner's lease is matched by lessor name and parcel. Check the Ownership tab row count against the opinion.
Is my data stored anywhere?
No database. Text-based PDFs never leave your browser — only their text is sent for analysis. Scanned PDFs are uploaded temporarily for OCR. Use "Save review session" if you want to keep your edits.
